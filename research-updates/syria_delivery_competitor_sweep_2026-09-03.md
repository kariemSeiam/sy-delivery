## Ranked Findings

### Tier 1 — Confirmed live product with real users (highest threat)

#### YallaGo — threat level UP since baseline
- **What's new:** Press reporting YallaGo has crossed **1 million users** "across most major provinces," with quotes from YallaGo's marketing/branding manager (Dalia al-Ghabra) to Levant24. Company is "considering expanding beyond ride-hailing" into **food and goods delivery, plus intercity travel booking**, aiming for a longer-term "integrated digital services ecosystem" and eventual expansion beyond Syria.
- Consistent with the already-known rebrand as "The Super App of Syria" (YallaMart / YallaVendor / YallaGo Safeer) and government backing via the Ministry of Communications; exhibited at LEAP 2026 Riyadh (Aug 31–Sep 3).
- **Why it matters for Bosla/Latakia:** This is the most credible super-app threat in the market right now — proven ride-hailing distribution (1M users) + government relationship + explicit intent to bolt on delivery. If YallaGo executes the delivery layer before Bosla launches, it inherits an existing user base rather than needing to build one.
- **Sources:**

  - Google Play listing shows continuous updates through Aug 24, 2026 (v1.48.08), ~289K downloads on third-party mirror trackers.

#### My Syria — NEW ENTRANT, not in baseline (confirmed live, tourism-focused today, delivery/ride-hailing on roadmap)
- Launched **July 29, 2026** by **121 Living** (UAE-based, founded by Syrian entrepreneurs Rami Kaiem, Feras Kaiem, Waseem Qudmani, Kinan Madi). Funded privately by the four co-founders (not the $10M Labby round — separate company).
- Live product today: hotel/apartment booking, airport taxi, car rental, restaurant booking. **Damascus-only** for now: ~45 restaurants, 20 hotels, 2 holiday-home buildings, 3 car rental companies. **15,000 downloads since July 29 launch.** New services rolling out every 1–2 weeks.
- **Critical detail for Bosla:** Founder Rami Kaiem told EnterpriseAM the expansion plan is **"Aleppo then Latakia, Tartus or Homs"** — Latakia is explicitly named as a next-wave city, and the roadmap includes ride-hailing and delivery as future built-out services beyond the current tourism-only slate.
- Backed/supported by the Syrian Tourism Ministry; tied to a broader 2026 tourism boom (3.52M visitors 1H 2026, +111% YoY).
- **Threat framing:** Not a direct delivery competitor today, but a super-app-track entrant with capital, government support, and an explicit Latakia expansion intent — should be tracked closely, not ignored just because it's "tourism" branded now.
